# WorldClock — HANDOFF (2026-11-18)

Edge-hidden world clock pill (PySide6). Slides out when the mouse hits the screen edge at its saved spot.
Cities: LA (CA hols), NY (NY hols), AU = Sydney (NSW), GE = Berlin (DE-BE). Sun by day, moon in the current phase by night (Sydney's moon is mirrored on purpose). Holiday = dimmed column + hover balloon.

- Files: worldclock.pyw (window/edge/drag/tray/autostart), ui.py (painting), clockdata.py (times/sun/moon/holidays).
- Python: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Python\Python313 (holidays, astral, tzdata added).
- Settings: %APPDATA%\WorldClock\settings.json. Autostart: HKCU Run\WorldClock (tray toggle).
- Debug: `--screenshot out.png`, `--force-holiday GE`, `--force-night`.

Status: v1 built and rendered; hot zone checked in code only. Not yet tested by hand: dragging, the hover balloon, the tray menu.
Liquid reveal (2026-11-18): the slide is replaced by liquid.py. A black drop drips off the edge (gooey neck that pinches and snaps), lands, and spreads into the pill with a spring wobble; the content fades in column by column. Hide runs it in reverse and sucks the drop back into the edge. Show is about 750 ms of visible motion, hide about 470 ms, and it reverses mid-way. The window stays fixed at the edge and all motion is in paintEvent. Tuning constants are at the top of liquid.py.
Reverted to v2 (the spring version) because Mario preferred it. It keeps three fixes from v3: font warm-up at start, content clipped to the shape, and a frame timer that matches the display refresh rate.
The eased v3 (one progress curve, paint-string detach, speed.png) is saved in _versions\v3_easecurve; the current v2 files are in _versions\v2_spring.
`--frames DIR [--edge bottom]` writes 26 PNGs, contact.png, drip.png, reveal.mp4 (60 fps) and reveal.gif (50 fps), and checks for clipping. The output is in _shots\anim. Not yet checked by hand: how the reveal feels live, and clicks passing through the transparent window area.
v4 settings (2026-11-18): 'Settings…' is in the tray menu and on the pill's right-click. It is a dark frameless dialog (settings.py) for 1-8 clocks: reorder, 3-letter label, per-clock holiday toggle, and search across 308 cities. Changes apply live, and the pill stays pinned open while the dialog is up.
City data: cities.json is built and validated by tools/build_cities.py (tz, zone.tab country, offset vs longitude, astral coordinates, holiday subdivisions). settings.json now has a 'clocks' list; old files migrate to LA/NY/AU/GE. The pill width scales with the clock count and liquid.py is unchanged. Debug: `--clocks N`, `--settings-shot PNG [--search TEXT]`. Saved in _versions\v4_settings.
v5 animation modes (2026-11-18): the settings dialog has an Animation control (Liquid / Slide / None); it applies live, replays a preview on the pill, and is saved in settings.json as "anim" (default liquid). Slide (anim_modes.py) is a soft-capped spring in (SLIDE_FREQ 3.2 Hz, ZETA 0.55, CAP 7 px, about 530 ms) and an ease-in out (SLIDE_HIDE_ACCEL 2600, about 270 ms). None appears and disappears instantly. liquid.py is unchanged.
`--frames DIR --anim slide|none|liquid` renders each mode; the slide renders are in _shots\anim_slide. Saved in _versions\v5_anim_modes.
v6 Show for: settings slider (0.5-10 s, steps 0.5/1/1.5/2/3/4/5/7/10, default 0.5) sets how long the pill stays after the mouse leaves; saved as "hide_delay" in settings.json, applies live. Saved in _versions\v6_showfor.
v8 monitors: a Monitors option (One / All) is saved as "monitors". In All mode a ClockSet keeps one pill per screen at a shared position (edge + x_frac, the pill centre as a fraction of the screen width) and adds/removes pills when screens change. Hot zones only fire on outer desktop edges. The old pixel "x" is replaced by x_frac.
Reset position (tray) = primary screen, top, centred, which is also the fresh-install default. The pill right-click menu (styled dark) has Settings / Reset to centre (that pill's screen). Saved in _versions\v8_monitors.
Known weak spot: an edge shared with another monitor (fast passes can be missed).

v9 Glass (2026-11-18): Settings > Style (Dark / Glass, saved as "style", default dark). Glass (glass.py) excludes the pill and balloon from capture (WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE; verified live) and BitBlts the screen under it in a worker thread at 30 fps, only while shown. It then refracts the grab with a squircle-bevel Snell lens (SDF: analytic capsule, cv2 distance transform while the liquid morphs), plus RGB dispersion, frost, a specular rim, an inner shadow and light/dark ink chosen from the backdrop luminance. Research notes are in docs/liquid_glass_notes.md.
CPU: 2 pills shown and settled = 2.7 % of one core; hidden = 0 %; about 3 ms per glass frame while animating. Side effect: in Glass mode the pill doesn't show up in screenshots or screen recordings. Debug: `--style glass --bg IMG` with --screenshot / --frames; renders are in _shots\glass_*.png and _shots\anim_glass. Saved in _versions\v9_glass.
Known nits: a small dark crease at the liquid neck while it re-attaches during hide. Not yet seen live by Mario.
v10 Glass effects (2026-11-18): Settings > Glass effects has three toggles, all off by default and saved as glass_materialize / glass_merge / glass_pointer. They are greyed out ("Glass style only") in Dark style. The code is in glass_fx.py. Materialize wraps any reveal and ramps lens, dispersion, frost, rim and tint from 0 to 1 with a 0.96 scale settle (250 ms with None). Merge replaces the animation: one glass blob per city, smooth-min union, a spring merge from the centre outward, and a split back into the edge on hide. Pointer light: the rim light and a glow follow the cursor; a press squishes the pill and sends a ripple.
CPU (2 monitors): settled 2.5 %; hovering with Pointer light 14 % of one core, capped at 60 fps; hidden 0 %. Glass and Dark renders with the effects off are pixel-identical to v9. Debug: `--fx materialize,merge` with --frames, and `--pointer-shots DIR --bg IMG`. Renders: _shots\anim_materialize, _shots\anim_merge, _shots\pointer. Saved in _versions\v10_glass_motion.
Not yet felt live by Mario: Merge timing (STAGGER, MERGE_* in glass_fx.py) and how strong the pointer glow is.
v11 Liquid SDF (2026-11-18): Liquid is now liquid_sdf.py. The physics are unchanged from v2 (it subclasses liquid.Liquid), but the drop is smooth-unioned with a reservoir slab at the screen edge, so the bulge, meniscus, neck, snap and swallow all come from one blend radius k. k = 2*SNAP_GAP while hanging, so the waist reaches zero exactly at the snap; after that k springs to 0. Each frame the SDF becomes 1-px AA coverage: in Dark it is filled with the black fill, rim and gloss; in Glass it goes through glass.paint_sdf. The settled pill is still drawn the classic way and is pixel-identical to v10.
The v2 path version is kept as anim "liquid_classic" (`--anim liquid_classic` or "anim" in settings.json; not in the UI). Settings > In speed (0.5x-2x, default 1x, saved as "in_speed") scales show time only, for every mode, by scaling dt in PillWidget.step_model. Debug: `--in-speed X`. Renders: _shots\anim_liquid2_dark (incl. pinch_8ms.png), _shots\anim_liquid2_glass, _shots\liquid_ab.png. Saved in _versions\v11_liquid_sdf.
CPU during a 1 s reveal: Dark 9-12 %, Glass 27 % of one core; hidden 0 %. After the hourglass a thin thread holds about 40 ms, then snaps and recoils (THREAD_* in liquid_sdf.py); hide mirrors it. Strips: _shots\anim_liquid2_dark\pinch_8ms.png and pinch_hide_8ms.png.
